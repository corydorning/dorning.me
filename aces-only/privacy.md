# Aces Only — Privacy Policy

_Effective October 14, 2026_

Aces Only is a golf game for iPhone made by Cory Dorning ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to information when
you play it. In short: I don't run any servers and I don't collect your personal information. The game uses three services
from Apple and Google, described below, and each has its own privacy policy.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your progress, stats, settings, Daily results and achievements are saved only on your iPhone, in the game's own storage.
They aren't sent to me. Deleting the game deletes them.

## Game Center (Apple)

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game posts your scores (holes played, holes in one, hole-in-one percentage, longest
streak, mulligans used, and your Daily aces and streak) to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them
with your Game Center name. The game also shows the leaderboards, including your friends' scores, inside the game. Game
Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad before using a mulligan, and one after every six holes.
To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and the
approximate location it gives, device type, operating system, crash and performance data, and how you interact with ads.

- **Tracking:** iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising
  identifier to show more relevant ads. If you don't, you still see ads, just less personalized ones. You can change this
  at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Consent in Europe and the UK:** where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are
  shown.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Purchases (Apple)

Buying the game, to remove the ads, is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details.
The game only learns from Apple whether the purchase was made, so it can stop showing ads, and it can restore that purchase
on another device.

## Sharing your Daily result

When you tap Share after a Daily, iOS's share sheet opens with a short message (for example "Aces Only, Daily #2, 3/3").
You choose whether and where to send it; the game doesn't send it anywhere by itself.

## Children

Aces Only isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
