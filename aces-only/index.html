Hole-in-One Golf Challenge

Every hole is a par 3, and only a hole in one finishes it. Read the green, pull back, and drop it in.

How it plays

Only aces count
Pull back to set your power, aim, and let it fly. Miss, and take a mulligan. Ace all 18 to finish the round.
Three painted courses
Coastal Cove, Pine Ridge and Canyon Run: 54 hand-painted holes, each course with its own ground, from soft and slow to firm and fast.
A new Daily, every day
One hole for everyone and three tries to ace it. Share how you did.
Earn your Tour Card
Win trophies in Amateur to unlock Pro: a tighter cup and leaderboards of its own.
Game Center
Leaderboards for every course and achievements to chase.
