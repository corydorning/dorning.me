# Aces Only — Support

**Questions, a problem, or an idea?** Email cory@dorning.me and I'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**How do I remove the ads?** Tap REMOVE ADS under PLAY on the home screen, or on any ad's popup. It's a one-time purchase.

**I bought it before. How do I get it back?** Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

**Why didn't I get a mulligan?** A mulligan is earned by watching its ad to the end. If ads are blocked on your iPhone, or
you're offline, the ad can't play. Allow ads or connect to the internet, then tap Try again.

**How do I change my privacy choices?** In Europe and the UK: Settings → Privacy choices. Tracking: your iPhone's Settings →
Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**Where are my scores?** Your stats are on your iPhone (Stats on the home screen). Leaderboards use Game Center: sign in
through your iPhone's Settings → Game Center.
